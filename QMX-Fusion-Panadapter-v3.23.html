About the PC Receiver

Plays the radio through the PC from the same I/Q stream that draws the spectrum, so both run together. The radio's VOL and RF Gain don't change this sound; use Volume here. Unplug or turn down the radio's earphones if you hear both.

Output can be any Windows playback device, including a virtual audio cable (for example "CABLE Input") so other programs can use this audio. The list fills in once the panadapter is running; press REFRESH OUTPUT LIST if a device is missing.

While Listen on PC is ticked, STOP DISPLAY only freezes the spectrum and waterfall; the sound keeps playing. Press START DISPLAY to resume the picture, or untick Listen on PC to stop everything.

How Audio Stream works
                 
Audio Stream lets you listen to the radio through the PC. It records the receive audio coming from the QMX over USB, runs it through Audio Gain and the DSP filters on this tab, and plays it on the Output device. It also drives the Input Level meter and the Audio spectrum.

                 
Input: the QMX recording device (Windows Sound settings, Recording tab).
Output: your PC speakers or headphones, not the QMX.

                 
Start puts the radio in normal audio mode, so the Radio tab panadapter stops while Audio Stream runs. Stop, then press Start Display on the Radio tab to bring the panadapter back.
